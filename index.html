Cannon Fever: Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026  ·  Developer: Gamaedor  ·  Contact: info@cannonfever.com

What the game keeps

Your progress — levels reached, coins, hearts, power-ups, the cannon you chose and your settings — is stored only on your device. The game has no accounts, asks for no name, email or other personal information, and sends none of your game data to us or to anyone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Purchases

This version of the game sells nothing. There are no in-app purchases and no payment of any kind. Coins are earned by playing and spent inside the game.

Ads

The game shows a full-screen ad after every tenth completed level, and offers optional rewarded ads you may choose to watch — to triple a level's coins, to get extra cannonballs, or to get a heart back. Choosing not to watch costs you nothing but that reward. Ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ads software on your device may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier of your device (on Android, the Android advertising ID and the app set ID; on iPhone and iPad, Apple's Identifier for Advertisers, IDFA, only if you allow it, see below);
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, that an ad was shown or watched);
  • diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data.

This data goes to Google, not to us. We receive only anonymous, aggregated reports of how many ads were shown and what they earned — never anything that identifies a person. Google's use of the data is described at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings: on Android under Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads); on iPhone and iPad under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and > Apple Advertising.

iPhone and iPad

The first time you open the game, before any ad is requested, iOS shows Apple's “Allow ‘Cannon Fever’ to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites?” prompt. The game and Google's ads software may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you tap Allow. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the identifier is not available and you still get ads, chosen without it. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Google may also use Apple's SKAdNetwork, which reports that an ad led to an install without identifying you. Apple may collect crash and performance data about the game if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings; that is Apple's collection, under Apple's privacy policy.

Consent in Europe

Ads are shown through Google AdMob only where Google's consent requirements are satisfied. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, no personalised ads are shown unless consent has been obtained through Google's consent framework; where it has not been, no ads are shown at all.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, write to the address above and we will remove it.

Security

Data sent by Google's ads software travels encrypted (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date.
